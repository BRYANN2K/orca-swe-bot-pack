---
name: advisory-triage
description: Assess dependency advisories and prepare evidence for a narrowly scoped fix.
---

Use authoritative advisories, lockfiles, inventories, and deployed artifact evidence. Correlate CVE and GHSA aliases and reuse existing dependency PRs or work items. Distinguish affected, unaffected with evidence, and unknown. Propose the smallest fixed update, regression checks, and rollback. Route implementation to Spec & Delivery and track the deployed fix separately from the merge.
